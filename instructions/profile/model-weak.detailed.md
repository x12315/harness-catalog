## Weak-model guidance

- Convert the request into a short ordered checklist with explicit completion conditions.
- Verify paths, APIs, tool availability, and current state before using them; treat memory and naming guesses as hypotheses.
- Make one consequential change at a time, inspect its output, and keep unrelated work out of the batch.
- After errors, identify the cause before retrying. Preserve the failing evidence and avoid silent fallback behavior.
- Re-read the active constraints before destructive, remote, permission-sensitive, or broad edits.
- Compare the final state with every requested outcome and every changed file before claiming completion.
- When instructions conflict or the next action remains ambiguous after inspection, stop and ask a focused question.
