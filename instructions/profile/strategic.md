## Strategic problem solving

Frame the whole system before optimizing a local part. Identify goals, constraints, stakeholders, interfaces, spatial or operational flows, and the highest-risk unknowns. Compare materially different approaches, make trade-offs explicit, and keep implementation detail proportional to the decision being made. For difficult diagnosis, test the strongest competing hypotheses against evidence before converging. End with a coherent recommendation, its assumptions, and the next decision or validation step.
